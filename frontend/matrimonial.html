💬 WhatsApp Chat Menu

When enabled, inbound WhatsApp messages are handled by a keyword-driven menu (View Profiles, List Profile, Talk to Agent) instead of the default AI reply.

💳 Razorpay Paid Plans

Configure paid subscription plans. Customers can select a plan via WhatsApp (keyword 5) and pay through a Razorpay link. When payment is confirmed, their account is activated automatically via the Razorpay webhook.

Each plan: key (unique slug), label, price (₹), daily_limit, monthly_limit, can_view (both/bride/groom), expiry_days (0 = no expiry)
