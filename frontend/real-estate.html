Getting started
👥 Leads — capture from portals/website/WhatsApp/walk-ins, auto-scored and duplicate-checked. Set up Agent Routing to auto-assign by zone/project/language.
🏢 Inventory — tower → floor → unit availability, price lists (base + PLC + floor rise), and holds with an expiry timer.
📅 Site Visits — schedule viewings and log feedback for the conversion funnel.
🧾 Bookings — token amount → payment schedule → possession, with channel-partner commission auto-calculated.
🛠️ Post-Sales — complaint tickets and resale/renewal tracking for investor clients.
🤝 Channel Partners — broker KYC, commission slabs, and a self-serve partner portal link.
📁 Documents — RERA disclosures/brochures/floor plans, and the price-change audit trail.
