FAQ

Common questions

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Your store opens in any phone browser, and orders and questions go through WhatsApp, which your customers already use.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?

Yes, you connect a WhatsApp Business number during setup. We guide you through it after you sign up.

Can I choose my store link?

Yes. You pick the name in onshope.com/yourname during setup, as long as nobody else is using it.

Which currencies do you bill in?

India is billed in rupees (₹899 or ₹1,999 a month) and the UAE in dirhams (99 or 199 AED a month).

Can I upgrade or cancel later?

Yes. You can move from Small to Medium commerce or cancel from your billing settings at any time. See the refund policy for details.

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Use the Sign in button at the top. It's the same account for your store, inbox and orders.
