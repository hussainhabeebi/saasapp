Product Search — What to Share
When the bot identifies a matched product, what should it share?
Default keeps the existing behaviour: the bot shares the matched product's details and photos when a product is identified, and sends the category's photos when no specific product is found but the customer names a category. "Product only" strips back to just the product — no category photos in either case; if no product is found but a relevant category is detected, the bot suggests browsing that category by name instead. "Product + Category" goes the other way: when a product is matched, the bot also sends the category's photos alongside it, and still suggests the category by name when no match is found. In all modes, the once-per-customer-per-category dedup applies so category content never repeats.
💰 Zero Price Hallucinations
Only ever quote prices that exist in your catalog or business prompt
When on, every price the bot writes is checked against your product catalog and business prompt before the reply is sent. A price that isn't written there (a guessed price, a computed total or discount, a converted or rounded figure) is rejected and the bot is asked to answer again; if it still invents one, that sentence is removed and the customer is told your team will confirm the exact price. Off keeps the current behaviour.
Order Link on Product Questions
Share the checkout link when a customer asks about size/color/stock/price
Default is off: the bot only shares the checkout link once a customer clearly says they want to order (e.g. "order this", "I'll take it") — a size/color/stock/price question gets product details and a photo, no link, however confident the match. Turn this on to also let the bot share the link on a product question itself if it seems ready to help (e.g. "yes, we have that in size M" + link) — it still won't force the link into every reply, only when it fits.
Order Link Sending
When a customer is ready to order, how should the bot take it?
Default is on: the bot sends a checkout link once a customer confirms they want to order. Turn this off to keep the whole order inside WhatsApp instead — the bot asks what they'd like to order and their delivery address right there in chat, then logs the order for your team to confirm (same "pending" status a link-based order gets on the Orders page). Choose "Talk to sales team" to skip both — the bot tells the customer someone from your team will reach out, tags the chat for handover, and logs a pending order for your team to take over from there.

WhatsApp Templates (Meta)
Sent via your connected WhatsApp Business number when an order's status changes — straight to Meta's Graph API, no Chatwoot dependency. Connect WhatsApp under Settings → Channels first.
How it works:
Changing an order's status automatically sends the matching WhatsApp template to the customer, and also notifies your other connected systems.

• Status → received / confirmed / processing → sends the "Order Received" template
• Status → shipped / dispatched / out_for_delivery → sends the "Order Shipped" template
• Status → delivered / completed → sends the "Order Delivered" template

Note: this only fires on a status change — creating a new order already at that status won't trigger it.
Technical details
Changing an order's status here (or via NocoDB) fires a webhook on the ecom_orders table → after.update → POST to:
https://n8n.aiingo.com/webhook/leadvyne-ecom-order-event
