Clips

Multiple clips get stitched into one video, in this order.

1 Transcript & captions

Word-level, auto-detected language unless you set one — tap a word to fix it.
Dotted-border words are approximate timing (the transcription provider only returned sentence-level timestamps) — text is still accurate. Clear a word and click away to delete it; use "+ Add word" in a scene to insert one.
Free (MyMemory API) — word timing is approximated (evenly spread), not word-for-word synced, since translation changes word count/order.

2 Caption style

Applied to the burned-in captions in the final export, previewed live on the left.
Overrides the selected style's font for THIS project — type the exact family name of a .ttf/.otf you've added to the render pipeline's fonts folder (see its README). Leave blank to use the style's own font.

3 Auto-edit

Pick a template to pre-fill these — still overridable per-project below. Everything here autosaves and applies automatically when you hit Render in step 5 — no separate "apply" step.
✂️ Cut silences / filler words 🔎 Auto-zoom on emphasis 🫥 Text behind subject (beta) 🔇 Reduce background noise 🎯 Smart auto-reframe 🥁 Beat-synced cuts 🟩 Green screen
Beta — free local person-detection, approximate edges. Turns off silence-cut/auto-zoom/B-roll/SFX for this render (not combinable yet). Smart auto-reframe uses the same free local model to keep you in frame instead of a fixed center-crop. Beat-synced cuts need background music picked below.
A single speed for the whole clip, not a per-segment ramp.

4 B-Roll / SFX / VFX cues

Suggested from the script's keywords, free (no AI call) — review, check/uncheck what you want. Autosaves as you go; the Save button below is just a manual double-check.
